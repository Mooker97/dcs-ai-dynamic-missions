**Classification:** adapted scenario: real war, invented detachment and sortie · **Date/Time:** 19 SEP 1993, 1410L · **Callsign:** Merani 2-1 (Mi-8MTV2, single ship)

At 1320L Merani 2-2 (Capt. Dolidze) reported hits from a gun in the hills north-east of
Ochamchire on the way back from Babushara and made a forced landing in farmland between the coast
road and the Kodori mouth, about 6 NM north-west of Ochamchire. All four aboard are alive; one has
a leg injury. They have one handheld radio. The aircraft is not recoverable.

The gun is assessed as a ZU-23 position on the high ground toward the Tkvarcheli pocket, where
Abkhaz units have held out all year. Those units saw the helicopter go down. It is assessed that
they will send a patrol, on foot or with a BMP, down the Kodori road or out of the eastern hills.
We do not know where it is now or how long the crew has. Kolkhi assesses about an hour.

## 1. Situation

- **Enemy.** *Known:* one ZU-23 position (three guns) on the hills about 8 NM north-east of
  Ochamchire, shown on the map. *Possible:* a patrol (rifle section, possibly a BMP) moving toward
  the crash site from the Kodori road or the eastern hills; a Zviadist roadblock on the Zugdidi or
  Khobi road on the way home. No radar SAMs. *Most likely:* the patrol is still more than ten
  minutes away when you land. *Most dangerous:* the patrol reaches the treeline while you are on
  the ground.
- **Friendly.** Merani 2-2 crew (4, one injured) near the wreck, on the flight frequency, smoke
  on request. Kolkhi follows the flight. No other aircraft available.
- **Civilians.** Refugee traffic on the coast road through Ochamchire.

## 2. Mission

Merani 2-1 flies from Senaki this afternoon to the crash site north-west of Ochamchire, picks up
Merani 2-2's crew and returns them to Senaki.

## 3. Execution

- **Commander's intent.** Bring all four out before a patrol reaches them, without losing the
  second aircraft. End state: crew at Senaki, Merani 2-1 serviceable.
- **Concept of operations.**
  1. *Departure:* Senaki, low across Mingrelia to IP INGURI.
  2. *Ingress:* coast north-west over the water past Ochamchire. Stay seaward of the coast road.
  3. *Search:* inside the search circle Two-Two will call. Ask for smoke. Approach from the sea
     or the south, never from the north-east hills.
  4. *Pick-up:* land near the smoke, keep the engines running; the crew needs about 40 seconds.
  5. *Egress and recovery:* back out over the sea, then Senaki, avoiding the Zugdidi and Khobi
     roads.
- **Coordinating instructions.**
  - ROE: return fire only on positively identified armed enemies. Not over Ochamchire.
  - Altitude: below 300 ft AGL over land, 100-200 ft over the sea.
  - Abort: patrol in contact at the landing site and the crew not visible → lift, call Two-Two,
    re-attack from the south once. Engine damage → Senaki.
  - Time limit: if Two-Two goes silent (about 50 minutes after launch), Kolkhi will call you
    home. Do not search the hills alone.
  - Bingo: 900 kg at the crash site.

## 4. Administration and logistics

- **Loadout:** empty cabin, medic aboard, flares, no rocket pods (DCS default payload).
- **Fuel:** full internal. **Start:** ramp cold at Senaki, or the low air-start variant.
- **Divert:** Kutaisi. **Medical:** doctor on the Senaki pad.

## 5. Command and signal

| Agency | Callsign | Frequency |
|---|---|---|
| Flight / Senaki ops | Kolkhi | 251.0 AM |
| Downed crew | Merani 2-2 | 251.0 AM |
| Senaki tower | Senaki | 261.0 AM / 132.0 AM |

- **Codewords:** "Smoke" = Two-Two marks the landing site; "Four of four" = all crew aboard.
- **Authentication:** Two-Two answers with his engineer's first name.
- **Lost comms:** fly to the search circle, look for smoke, land only if the crew is visible.
