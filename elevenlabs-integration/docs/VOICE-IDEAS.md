## Voice Design Prompts by Role

### 1. AWACS Controller (Magic)

**Role:** Early Warning & Control aircraft controller providing tactical picture and threats

**When to Use:**
- Mission start briefing
- Enemy aircraft warnings
- SAM warnings
- Weather/visibility updates
- Mission complete

**Voice Characteristics:**
- Professional, calm under pressure
- Clear enunciation for radio
- Technical but not robotic
- Authoritative without being aggressive
- Steady pacing, no urgency unless critical

**ElevenLabs Prompt:**
```
Professional male AWACS controller, age 35-45, American accent with neutral/Midwest tone, highly trained military radio voice with perfect clarity and measured cadence. Calm and composed even during emergencies, technical vocabulary delivered naturally, authoritative presence without aggression. Clear diction for critical radio communications, steady breathing, confident tone from years of air battle management experience.
```

**Alternative (Female):**
```
Professional female AWACS controller, age 32-42, American accent with neutral tone, trained military radio voice with exceptional clarity and controlled delivery. Calm professionalism during high-stress situations, technical precision in communications, authoritative but warm presence. Clear enunciation for long-range radio, steady pacing, confident tone from extensive air battle management training.
```

## Tips for Creating Voices

### Voice Design Best Practices:

1. **Be Specific:**
   - Age range (narrow it down)
   - Accent type (region/country)
   - Emotional state
   - Experience level
   - Vocal quality

2. **Include Context:**
   - Military role and experience
   - Combat environment
   - Communication style
   - Stress level
   - Professional bearing

3. **Audio Quality:**
   - Always mention "perfect audio quality" or "clear radio voice"
   - Specify if radio-filtered or direct
   - Mention clarity requirements

4. **Test and Iterate:**
   - Generate sample with test phrase
   - Listen to several scenarios
   - Adjust prompt if needed
   - Regenerate until satisfied

### Sample Test Phrases:

**Calm:**
- "All callsigns, mission is a go. Good hunting."
- "Package is complete. RTB."

**Urgent:**
- "We need air support, NOW!"
- "SAM launch! Missile in the air!"

**Professional:**
- "Target coordinates grid november papa four-five-six."
- "Splash one. Target destroyed."
