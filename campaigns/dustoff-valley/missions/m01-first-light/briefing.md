# FIRST LIGHT

**Fictional scenario** · **11 APR, 0610L (D+2)** · **Dusty 1-1, AH-64D, single ship** · **Hatay**

## Intelligence narrative — Capt. P. Nand, S2

Two days ashore and we still know very little about the valley. What we have comes from Kestrel
Two-Two, who has been watching from the ridge north of the canal for a week, and from what the
enemy chose to show us when the first helicopters came over the airfield. Two gun sites, one at
Reyhanli and one south of Kirikhan, fired on them, so we know where those are. We have no idea
what did not fire.

Northern Valley Command has had months to prepare. It is short of radar, so assume guns and
shoulder-launched missiles sited by eye on the approaches you would choose yourself. The militias
in the farmland work for NVC and report helicopter movements by phone; expect technicals and small
foot patrols that scatter when engaged. Kestrel also reports trucks coming and going at night from
a walled farm in the north-east corner of the box. We do not know what they carry.

Civilians are still on the roads. Their trucks look like the militia's trucks. Positive
identification is the whole job this morning.

## 1. Situation

- **Enemy.** Known AAA (ZU-23 class) at Reyhanli and south of Kirikhan, both static. Militia
  technicals and AK teams likely in and around the box. Possible unlisted guns. No radar SAMs
  expected. *Most likely:* the militia avoids contact and reports your position. *Most
  dangerous:* an unlisted gun sited on your patrol line, cued by militia spotters.
- **Friendly.** Kestrel Two-Two, a four-man observation team, is on the high ground north of the
  canal with eyes over the box. No other friendly forces east of the ridge. No CAS on call.
- **Civilians.** Farm and town traffic on the canal road between Reyhanli and Kirikhan.

## 2. Mission

At 0630L Dusty 1-1 conducts an armed reconnaissance of the patrol box east of Reyhanli to
establish enemy disposition and air-defence locations ahead of the push inland.

## 3. Execution

- **Commander's intent.** I want a picture, not a medal. Purpose: find out what is in the valley
  before we commit anyone to it. Key tasks: fly the box, report every contact by type and
  location, locate unlisted air defence. End state: Dusty back on deck with a picture and an
  aircraft we can fly tomorrow.
- **Concept.** (1) Depart Hatay, push east to the gate west of Reyhanli, passing between the two
  known gun sites. (2) Fly the box: SW, NW, NE, SE, back to SW. (3) Exit through the gate. (4)
  Recover to Hatay.
- **Coordinating instructions.**
  - ROE: weapons free on positively identified armed targets only. Unarmed vehicles are not
    targets, whatever they look like.
  - Stand off at least 2 NM from the known sites. You may engage them at your discretion, but
    this is not a strike mission.
  - Abort: aircraft damage affecting handling, or a radar SAM indication. Turn west and come home.
  - Kestrel's calls are information, not clearance. You are the engagement authority.

## 4. Administration & logistics

- **Loadout:** 8x AGM-114K, FCR, full 30 mm. No rockets.
- **Start:** ramp cold, Hatay. **Divert:** none; Hatay is the only friendly field.
- **Fuel:** full internal; plan 45 minutes from push to recovery.
- **CSAR:** none available east of the ridge. Do not get shot down.
