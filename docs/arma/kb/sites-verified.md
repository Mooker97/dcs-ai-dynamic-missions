# Altis sites verified in Arma 3

**Verified 2026-10-27** on Arma 3 2.22, dedicated server, Altis. Source run: the diag mission
`tools/arma_harness/diag/dms_diag.Altis`, generated by `tools/arma_harness/make_diag.py`.

## How

1. **Old candidates.** The diag logged each site centre and QRF origin from `fn_config.sqf`. It
   recorded `surfaceIsWater`, `getTerrainHeightASL`, `isOnRoad`, houses within 150 m
   (`nearestObjects [p, ["House","Building"], 150]`) with their `buildingPos` count, the nearest road
   (`nearRoads 1500`), the nearest town (`nearestLocations [p, ["NameVillage","NameCity","NameCityCapital"], 6000]`),
   `surfaceNormal` and terrain blockers and trees within 100 m.
2. **Military structure scan.** It ran `nearestObjects [[15000,15000,0], <cargo HQ/house/patrol/tower,
   barracks, bag bunkers>, 25000]`, which found 312 objects, and logged their positions. These were
   clustered offline at 120 m. Small outposts with 3–6 structures, more than 1 km from a town and close
   to a road, were kept.
3. **Candidate sweep.** For each candidate it listed the cargo structures within 90 m
   (`buildingPos`) and the terrain wall and fence types within 60 m. It then sampled a ring of
   start/LZ points every 30° at 350 m and 1000 m: water, slope, trees within 50 m, houses within 100 m,
   and line of sight from the compound (`terrainIntersectASL`).
4. **Real mission check.** The mission was run in test mode on each site (see `docs/arma/TESTING.md`).

garrisons every `Land_Cargo_*_V1_F` within 60 m. It places the intel table, camo net, ammo box,

it has no terrain building, wall, fence, tree or rock within the clear radius, `surfaceNormal` z is
above 0.95 and it is not water.
