# Premise — Viper Detachment

## Pitch

A small USAF F-16 detachment, "Rook", flies out of Incirlik in support of a partner ground force
holding the border plain north of Aleppo. The enemy is not an army: it is the Northern Plains
Front, a well-funded insurgency that looted a collapsed garrison's stocks at Kuweires and now
fields technicals, mortar teams, a few old tanks and, worst of all, a handful of captured radar
SAMs. Four sorties, four of the Viper's core jobs: take down the SAM belt, flatten a weapons
depot with laser-guided bombs, put bombs on fortified positions for a JTAC with troops in
contact, and finally hunt the last mobile SAM as it is moved under escort. It should feel like
work, not a movie.

## Interview answers

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| Tone | Grounded, procedural. "It doesn't need to be that fun, I want it to feel kinda realistic." Light story, no melodrama. |
| Era / year | 2016 (equipment of the mid-2010s). In-fiction calendar is invented; no real event is implied. |
| Realism | Grounded: cold starts, realistic loadouts and comms, ROE that matter, ordinary weather. |
| Mode | **fictional** (see Mode rationale) |
| Player aircraft & callsign | F-16C Block 50 (DCS `F-16C_50`), USAF. Flight lead of a 2-ship, callsign PYTHON 1-1; AI wingman PYTHON 1-2. |
| Theater / home base | Syria map. Home: Incirlik. Diverts: Adana Sakirpasa, Gaziantep, Hatay. |
| Scope | 4 missions, 60–90 min each (incl. cold start), single player. Linear: SEAD/DEAD → LGB strike → CAS → combined SEAD+interdiction. |
| Start choice | Every mission offers two player flights: a ramp (cold, parking) start at Incirlik, and an air start near the IP. Story beats never depend on takeoff. |
| Voice | Subtitles only for now. Voice profiles are written so ElevenLabs can be added later (no voice ids). |
| Hard no's | No real named people, units or operations. No re-enactment of the real Syrian war. No gore, no melodrama. |
| Priorities | SEAD/DEAD (HARM + HTS, pop-up SAMs) and laser-guided bombing (GBU-12/-10/-24, JTAC talk-ons and self-lase). No pure air-to-air CAP mission. |

## Inspirations

- The rhythm of real fighter-squadron work: mass brief, step, cold start, push, the long transit, a
  few minutes of intense work, the long way home, debrief.
- COIN air support: ROE, positive identification, collateral concern, and a JTAC who sees more
  than you do.

## Mode rationale

**fictional**. The Northern Plains Front, the Northern Border Brigade and the coalition task force
are invented, and so is the calendar. Real towns and airfields (Incirlik, Minakh, Al Bab, Kuweires,
Tell Rifaat, Manbij) keep their names as terrain only. "Adapted" would invite later agents to
anchor events to the real 2010s conflict, which the user explicitly does not want. Insurgents fly
the DCS `Insurgents` flag, their captured heavy weapons the `CJTF Red` flag, and the partner force
`CJTF Blue`, so no real nation's forces are depicted on the enemy or partner side.
