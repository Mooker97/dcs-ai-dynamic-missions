# Premise — Dustoff Valley

## Pitch

A small coalition Apache detachment has just come ashore and taken Hatay airfield. Beyond the
ridge lies the Amik valley: flat farmland, canals, two towns with guns in them, and an enemy
nobody has a clear picture of. Before the main push inland can start, someone has to go and
look. That someone is Dusty flight. Over five missions the valley goes from "light presence
expected" to a fight for the road the whole push depends on.

## Inspirations

- Baltic Dragon campaigns: story in the gaps around procedure.
- The user's prompts: "Report anything interesting. Weapons free on armed targets. Good hunting."

## Mode rationale

**fictional**, not adapted. The war, factions and political geography are invented: here the enemy
holds Reyhanli and Kirikhan, which are really Turkish towns. "Adapted" would invite later agents to
anchor events to a real operation that never happened. Only terrain is real, and it is sourced in
bible.yaml. Enemy factions fly DCS's CJTF Red / Insurgents flags, so no real nation is depicted.
