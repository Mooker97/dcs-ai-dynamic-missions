## Intelligence narrative

At 0800L a militia column came out of the hills and moved west on the valley road toward Zugdidi. A partner company, ANVIL, holds the road checkpoint east of the town with JTAC Bulldog One-Two.

Assessed: a lead group of BMPs or T-72s, covered by a ZSU-23-4 Shilka on the high ground north of the road. Igla teams are reported on the farms either side of the road. More vehicles may follow from the east.

Unknown: the exact mix of the column and how many follow-on vehicles there are.

## 1. Situation

- **Enemy.** Lead armour group on the valley road about 4 km east of the checkpoint. **ZSU-23-4** on the low hill north of the road. **Igla** MANPADS on the farms. Possible follow-on BTRs/BMPs 9 km east. No enemy air.
- **Friendly.** ANVIL at the checkpoint only. Bulldog One-Two is the only terminal controller. Darkstar (E-3) has the area.

## 2. Mission

From 0930L HAWG provides close air support to Bulldog One-Two east of Zugdidi to stop the column before it reaches the checkpoint.

## 3. Execution

- **Commander's intent.** Stop the column short of the checkpoint without hitting the farms. End state: lead group destroyed, checkpoint held, both jets home.
- **Concept.** (1) Depart Senaki north (ramp) or start on the ingress axis (air). (2) IP MULE: Darkstar hands you to Bulldog. (3) Type 2: 9-line, cleared hot. Bulldog's marks show on the F10 map. (4) Off station on his release, RTB Senaki.
- **Coordinating instructions.** ROE: PID, release only on "cleared hot". The checkpoint is a **no-fire area**. Stay above 8,000 ft AGL until the Shilka is dead. Abort on undefeated SAM, damage or Bulldog's "abort": egress south. Bingo 3,000 lb.

## 4. Administration & logistics

- **Loadout (per jet, CAS preset):** ALQ-131 (1), LAU-131 7x M151 (2), 2x AGM-65D (3, 9), GBU-12 (4, 8), GBU-38 (5, 7), LITENING (10), 2x AIM-9M (11), full gun.
