# FIRST RUN

**Classification:** adapted scenario: real war, invented detachment and sortie · **Date/Time:** 17 SEP 1993, 0930L · **Callsign:** Merani 2-1 (Mi-8MTV2, single ship)

## Intelligence narrative — Capt. D. Japaridze, detachment S2

The ceasefire ended yesterday morning. Abkhaz units and North Caucasian volunteers attacked across
the Gumista line north-west of Sukhumi and in the hills north of the city. Our heavy guns were
withdrawn under the July agreement and have not come back. The garrison is holding, but it is
firing ammunition faster than the road can bring it, and the road south is filling with people
who have decided not to wait.

Babushara airfield is still open. Airliners are still landing there; we are told that may not
last. The besiegers have at least one gun position in the hills north of the field, confirmed by
the garrison yesterday. It is assessed that more guns will be moved forward at night to cover the
approach lanes. Their gunners shoot by eye, so the sea approach is the safe one.

On the way home you cross Mingrelia. Armed supporters of the former president hold roadblocks
between Zugdidi and Senaki and have fired on government vehicles this week. They are not part of
the war in Abkhazia, but they will shoot at a government helicopter. What we do not know is where
the next gun will be, and whether Granite will have fuel for you. Assume he will not.

## 1. Situation

- **Enemy.** *Known:* one ZU-23 position (two guns) in the hills about 5 NM north of Babushara,
  shown on the map. *Possible:* a second ZU-23 east of the field or at the Gumista mouth; a
  Zviadist roadblock (DShK technical, riflemen) on the Zugdidi or Khobi road. No radar SAMs.
  *Most likely:* sporadic gunfire on the approach and rockets on the airfield's west end.
  *Most dangerous:* a new gun under the approach lane while you are slow and heavy on final.
- **Friendly.** Granite (Sgt. Beridze) runs the loading party on the Babushara apron. Babushara
  tower (Capt. Abashidze) works the field on one radio. Kolkhi (Senaki ops) follows the flight.
  No escort, no CAS, no sister ship today.
- **Civilians.** Refugees on the coast road south-east of the airfield and crowding the
  terminal. They are never targets.

## 2. Mission

Merani 2-1 flies two tonnes of ammunition from Senaki to Babushara today, lands on the apron,
loads the refugees Granite brings out, and returns them to Senaki.

## 3. Execution

- **Commander's intent.** Deliver the ammunition and bring out a full load without losing the
  aircraft. One airframe is worth more than one sortie. End state: cargo delivered, refugees at
  Senaki, aircraft serviceable for tomorrow.
- **Concept of operations.**
  1. *Departure:* Senaki, west low level across Mingrelia to IP INGURI (coast in at Anaklia).
  2. *Ingress:* north-west along the coast, over the water, past Ochamchire.
  3. *Babushara:* contact tower at 7 NM, approach from the sea, land on the apron. Keep the
     engines running. Granite unloads, then loads; lift on his "go".
  4. *Egress:* back out over the sea to EGRESS OCHAMCHIRE. Flight engineer calls fuel.
  5. *Recovery:* Senaki, avoiding the Zugdidi and Khobi roads. Ambulances on the pad.
- **Coordinating instructions.**
  - ROE: return fire only on positively identified armed positions. Never over the coast road.
  - Altitude: below 300 ft AGL over land, 100-200 ft over the sea.
  - Abort: hydraulic or engine damage before Babushara → turn back to Senaki with the cargo.
  - Bingo: 1,100 kg before Babushara; below it, return without landing.
  - Time on the apron: as short as Granite allows, normally under two minutes.

## 4. Administration and logistics

- **Loadout:** 2,000 kg ammunition internal, no rocket pods, flares (DCS default payload).
- **Fuel:** full internal, the day's ration. No fuel planned at Babushara.
- **Start:** ramp cold at Senaki, or the air-start variant low over Mingrelia.
- **Divert:** Kutaisi (not reachable on homeward fuel; outbound only). **CSAR:** none.

## 5. Command and signal

| Agency | Callsign | Frequency |
|---|---|---|
| Flight / Senaki ops | Kolkhi | 251.0 AM |
| Babushara tower | Babushara | 258.0 AM / 129.0 AM |
| Senaki tower | Senaki | 261.0 AM / 132.0 AM |
| Babushara apron | Granite | on tower frequency |

- **Codewords:** "Granite" = apron party ready; "Doors closed" = lift.
- **Lost comms:** continue to Babushara if the approach is clear; land on the apron and wait for
  hand signals; return the same way.
