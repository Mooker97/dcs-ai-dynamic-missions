## GBU-12 CHEAT SHEET (self-lase)
1. MASTER ARM: ARM. LASER ARM: ARM (MISC panel). RIGHT HDPT: ON (pod power)
2. ICP **A-G** button (A-G master mode)
3. Left MFD **SMS**: GBU-12, mode **PRE** (OSB toggles PRE/VIS)
4. Right MFD **TGP**: out of STBY to A-G. **DMS down** = pod is SOI
5. Slew the cross onto the target (cursor), **TMS up** = track (again = point)
6. Pod code: DED LIST, 0 MISC, 5 LASR, TGP CODE 1688. Bomb code is 1688 as loaded
7. HUD: fly the **steering line**, wings level. **Pickle and HOLD** until it releases
8. Laser fires on its own at LASER ST TIME before impact (LASR page)
9. Or lase by hand: trigger, first detent, pod SOI. Flashing **L** on the TGP page = lasing
10. After release: gentle turn of 30 deg or less, target ahead of the wing line. Keep the cross on it to impact
11. No effect? Check LASER ARM, the codes, that the pod was tracking, and that you didn't bank past the pod

**Self-defence:** CMS aft = chaff and flare program · ALQ-184 on the ECM panel
