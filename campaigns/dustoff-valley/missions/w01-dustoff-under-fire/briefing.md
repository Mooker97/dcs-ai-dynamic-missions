# DUSTOFF UNDER FIRE

**Fictional scenario** · **12 APR 2012, 0640L (D+3)** · **Watch mission: all AI, no player slot** · **About 13 minutes**

> You are watching, not flying. Dusty 1-1 (AH-64D), Dustoff 3-1 (UH-60A) and Venom 1-1
> (two F-16Cs) fly the whole thing on their own. Camera hints appear on screen as `CAMERA: ...`.

## Intelligence narrative — Capt. P. Nand, S2

At first light one of Kestrel Two-Two's team was hit while moving between positions on the ridge
north of the canal. Kestrel calls him urgent surgical: he needs an operating table within the hour.
The hide is compromised, and the militia in the farmland know a helicopter will come for him.

Kestrel has watched a walled compound on the canal road, four miles south of the ridge, for a
week. Militia technicals use it as their base. If they drive up the canal road they reach the foot
of the ridge in about ten minutes, and from there they can cover the LZ.

**Duration:** about 13 minutes from start to end (one variant: everything starts airborne).

**Stakes:** if Dustoff cannot get in, Kestrel's man waits until dark, and he may not have that long.

## 1. Situation

- **Enemy.** Two to four militia technicals (DShK pickups, sometimes a ZU-23 pickup) at the
  compound on the canal road, 4 NM south of the ridge. Trucks being loaded inside the compound.
  Possible (about one run in two): a small infantry team (RPG or machine gun) in the treeline
  1 NM east of the LZ. Possible (about one in three): a ZU-23 gun truck on the farm tracks south of
  Dustoff's egress. **Cue:** Kestrel calls each one 35-40 s before it appears, and it is marked on
  F10 (MOVEMENT / GUN TRUCK). No radar SAMs.
- **Friendly.** Kestrel Two-Two (UK observation team) on the ridge. Dusty 1-1 escorts. Venom 1-1,
  two F-16Cs with laser-guided bombs, holds 12 NM south of Hatay at 18,000 ft.

## 2. Mission

Dustoff 3-1 lifts Kestrel's casualty from the ridge LZ and clears the valley westbound.

## 3. Execution

1. Both helicopters start airborne 7-8 NM west of the ridge. Dusty pushes ahead; Dustoff holds at
   the gate 3 NM west of the LZ.
2. Dusty meets the technicals on the canal road and calls the LZ cold.
3. Ops clears Venom 1-1 to bomb the compound before a second push leaves it.
4. Dustoff lands, takes the casualty aboard (about 90 s on the ground), lifts, and egresses west.
   Dusty escorts. The mission ends when Dustoff is clear of the valley.

**What changes each time:** how many technicals, a hot or a cold LZ, a gun truck on the way out
or not, and where the bombs land.

## 4. Support

Ops: Hatay Ops, 251.0 AM (all aircraft). Kestrel Two-Two: 41.5 FM.

## 5. Command and signal

All flights share one net, so you hear everything. **Abort:** if Dustoff is lost, Ops launches
CSAR and the mission ends as a failure. **Bingo:** not a factor; the mission ends at 14:30 at the
latest. The mission ends automatically about 20 s
after Dustoff reaches the egress point.
