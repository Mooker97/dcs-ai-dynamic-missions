# Premise

## Pitch

"A10C CAS over Caucasus, 1 mission." HAWG 1-1, a two-ship of A-10C IIs out of Senaki-Kolkhi, is
scrambled to break up an armoured column pushing on a partner-force checkpoint east of Zugdidi.
One sortie, no campaign: the story lives in the radio work with the JTAC.
