# Range Day

**Classification:** fictional training material · **Date/Time:** 10 NOV 2016, 0900L · **Callsign:** Python 1-1 (single ship)
**Duration:** hot ramp start, about 60-70 min flight time
**Stakes:** if the compound keeps firing, Jackal's patrol has to pull back off the plain. Miss on the range and the card stays unsigned; the mission still goes on.

Welcome back. Before Rook schedules you for combat sorties again, you fly a laser-guided bomb requalification on Range Seven: two GBU-12 passes, one bomb each, coached by the range controller, Anvil. Take your time on the range. Anvil calls every switch.

You also carry two bombs on alert for close air support. NBB patrols have been in contact with Northern Plains Front technicals in the farm compounds north of Tell Rifaat all week. Assessed: if one goes in today, Rook Ops will retask you straight off the range.

Not known: which compound the fighters are using, and whether they bring a second technical.

## 1. Situation

**Enemy (live tasking only).** NPF fighters with a KORD heavy machine gun technical in one of three farm compounds south of Jackal's village. One **ZU-23 technical** covers the compounds: no RWR cue, you see tracers. Effective below about 8,000 ft AGL. A second MG technical may arrive after the clearance; Jackal will call it. No SAMs, no enemy air.

**Range Seven.** Inert targets on the Ceyhan delta, 18 NM south of Incirlik: Target 1 is a truck line on the west side, Target 2 is a tank hulk one mile east of it. Nothing on the range shoots.

**Friendly.** Anvil runs the range. Rook Ops owns the alert tasking. Jackal Two-Three (USAF JTAC) is with an NBB patrol in the village north of the compounds. **The village is no-strike.**

**Other traffic (no tasking with you).** Magic (E-3) orbits about 60 NM north-east of Incirlik. Colt 1-1 (2x F-15C) flies CAP over the border south of Gaziantep at 25,000 ft. Venom 3-1 (2x F-16C) departs runway 05 as you start up and patrols north of Kilis at 22,000 ft, about 10 NM north of your transit. Viper 2-1 (2x F-16C) recovers to Incirlik from the east about 15 minutes in, so watch for it if you're slow to taxi. C-130s fly the Incirlik pattern all sortie.

## 2. Mission

Python 1-1 requalifies on GBU-12s on Range Seven, then on retask destroys the NPF fighters firing on Jackal's patrol near Tell Rifaat, and recovers to Incirlik.

## 3. Execution

**Intent.** Get your hands back on the switches without pressure, then put a real bomb on the right compound with nothing near the patrol.

**Concept.**
- Departure: hot start, taxi runway 05 after Venom 3-1 has rolled, then turn south. The range opens when you are within 15 NM of it.
- Range: Anvil coaches the setup, then clears you hot on Target 1, then Target 2. Run in from IP ANVIL, north to south. Release around 15,000 ft and 450 kt. If you don't score within 6 minutes, Anvil moves you on.
- Retask: Rook Ops sends you to CP DUST, about 80 NM east (about 11 min). Jackal checks in on the same frequency. Call him at 15 miles, and inside 15 NM of the compounds he gives a 9-line in two parts, then clears you hot.
- Live attack: self-lase, code 1688, attack heading west, egress west to EGRESS. Stay above 12,000 ft.
- Recovery: Rook Ops calls you home. Incirlik Tower is on 360.1.

**Coordinating instructions.**
- ROE: on the range, Anvil's "cleared hot". Live: Jackal's "cleared hot" and PID on the pod. Nothing within 1 km of the village.
- Altitude block on the range: 12,000-20,000 ft. Live: 12,000 ft or higher.
- Abort: hit with damage, or the pod fails. Joker 5,000 lb, bingo 4,000 lb to Incirlik.
- If Jackal's patrol breaks contact (10 min after the clearance, or 20 min after check-in if you're not there yet), the tasking is cancelled and you RTB.

## 4. Administration & logistics

- Loadout: 4x GBU-12 (two TERs), LITENING pod, 2x 370 gal tanks, ALQ-184, 2x AIM-120C, 2x AIM-9X. Two bombs for the range, two for the alert.
- Start: hot on the Incirlik ramp. Engines and systems are running; check MASTER ARM, LASER ARM and RIGHT HDPT before the range.
- Diverts: Adana Sakirpasa (8 NM WSW of Incirlik), Hatay.
- Weapons cheat sheet: kneeboard page 2.

## 5. Command & signal

Two radios, no retuning: leave **UHF (COM1) on Incirlik Tower** and **VHF (COM2) on the tactical net** all sortie. Every UHF preset is 360.1 and every VHF preset is 138.0.

| Radio | Agencies | Frequency | Preset |
|---|---|---|---|
| UHF COM1 | Incirlik Tower, Venom 3-1, Viper 2-1 (airfield traffic) | 360.1 AM | UHF ch 1 |
| VHF COM2 | Anvil, Rook Ops, Jackal Two-Three, Magic, Colt 1-1 | 138.0 AM | VHF ch 1 |

Laser code **1688** all sortie. If you lose comms on the range, cease attacks, hold at IP ANVIL at 15,000 ft for 2 min, then RTB.
