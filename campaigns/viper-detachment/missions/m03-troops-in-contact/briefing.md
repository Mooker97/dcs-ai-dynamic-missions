# TROOPS IN CONTACT

**Fictional scenario** · **08 NOV, 1500L** · **PYTHON, 2x F-16C Block 50** · **Incirlik** · **Bullseye: Minakh**

**Pick your start.** Copy `m03-troops-in-contact-ramp.miz` for a cold start on the ramp at Incirlik, or `m03-troops-in-contact-air.miz` for an air start: 20,000 ft, 420 kt, 20 NM short of CP TUSK (the IP) on the Incirlik-TUSK axis, heading about 110. Either way you are **Python 1-1** in the cockpit and Pike flies **Python 1-2** as your AI wingman. The story starts at TUSK.

## Intelligence narrative — Rook S2

The NPF has answered our first week with a ground push. Since 1100L an NBB company, LANTERN, has been in contact in the village 6 km north of Tell Rifaat, with Jackal Two-Three. Fire comes from farm compounds south of the village. Technicals are moving north out of Tell Rifaat.

Assessed: a raid in strength, with Garrison armour (T-55s or BMPs) and a ZSU-23-4 at the north edge of Tell Rifaat. MANPADS reported on the farms. A short-range SAM (SA-9/SA-13 class) is assessed near Tell Rifaat, not located.

Unknown: which compounds are occupied, how many technicals are coming, how long LANTERN holds. Jackal estimates twenty minutes once air is overhead. Families are leaving on the technicals' road.

## 1. Situation

- **Enemy.** RPG teams and DShK technicals in compounds 0.5-3.5 km south of the village (Jackal's names: the Mill, the Orchard, the farms). Armour and a **ZSU-23-4**, north edge of Tell Rifaat. **Igla** teams on the farms. **Possible SA-9/SA-13** south of Tell Rifaat. No enemy air. *Most likely:* pressure until the company breaks. *Most dangerous:* a pop-up SAM or MANPADS against a jet that descends to find a target.
- **Friendly.** LANTERN holds the village only. Jackal Two-Three is the only terminal controller. Magic (E-3) has the area; Shell (KC-135) is on the AR track east of Incirlik.
- **Civilians.** Northbound cars on the main road through the village. The pickups look like technicals.

## 2. Mission

From 1500L, PYTHON provides close air support to Jackal Two-Three north of Tell Rifaat to break the NPF assault on LANTERN before the company is forced to withdraw.

## 3. Execution

- **Commander's intent.** Break the assault without hurting the people we are here to protect. Work Jackal's targets in his order, starting with the Mill. End state: assault broken, LANTERN in the village, no friendly or civilian casualties from our weapons.
- **Concept.** (1) Ramp start: depart runway 23. Air start: you are already on the ingress axis. (2) CP TUSK: Magic hands you to Jackal. (3) Type 2: 9-line, talk-on, cleared hot. Jackal's marks show on the F10 map. (4) Off station on his release, RTB Incirlik.
- **Coordinating instructions.**
  - ROE: PID required. Release only on Jackal's "cleared hot". The village is a **no-fire area**.
  - Danger close (the Orchard, about 500 m from LANTERN): pass initials. GBU-12 only, attack heading 270, parallel to the friendly line.
  - Altitude: stay above 12,000 ft AGL south of the Mill (Shilka, MANPADS). Cloud base is 7,000-9,000 ft.
  - Laser code 1688. Self-lase with the Litening.
  - Abort: a SAM launch you can't defeat, battle damage, or Jackal's "abort". Exit west to TUSK.
  - Bingo: 4,000 lb at TUSK.
