# WARDEN IN CONTACT

**Fictional scenario** · **14 APR 2012, 1530L (D+5)** · **Watch mission: all AI, no player slot** · **About 16 minutes**

> You are watching, not flying. Dusty 1-1 (two AH-64Ds), Dustoff 3-1 (UH-60A) and Venom 1-1
> (two F-16Cs) fly the whole thing on their own, and Warden's soldiers fight on the ground.
> Camera hints appear on screen as `CAMERA: ...`.

## Intelligence narrative — Capt. P. Nand, S2

Warden Three-One, a US mounted reconnaissance patrol, went east of the Amik valley two days ago
to watch the road across the Idlib plain. This afternoon militia caught the dismounts in the open
fields between Binnish and Taftanaz. They are in an irrigation ditch with two wounded and the
enemy is behind a farm wall five hundred metres east. Their three HMMWVs (Warden Three-Two) are
firing over them from three hundred metres back.

The plain is flat and open. That is good for the Apaches and bad for anyone on foot. NVC keeps a
garrison at Taftanaz airfield, three miles east, and it has armour. Reaper is overhead and will see
a quick reaction force leave. We expect missile teams to come with it.

**Duration:** about 15-18 minutes from start to end (one variant: everything starts airborne).

**Stakes:** if Dustoff cannot get in, Warden's wounded wait for a ground relief that has to fight
its way through the same QRF.

## 1. Situation

- **Enemy, in contact.** Two or three militia groups (riflemen, RPGs, sometimes a KORD machine gun)
  on the farm wall 540 m east and in the orchard 600 m north-north-east of Warden. **Cue:** marked
  on F10 at the start (ENEMY - FARM WALL).
- **Enemy QRF.** From the Taftanaz garrison: one or two armour groups (BTR-80s and/or BMP-2s), one
  air-defence vehicle (a ZSU-23-4 Shilka or a ZU-23 on a Ural truck), and about half the time a
  truck serial with a fuel bowser. It leaves either almost at once or about three minutes in.
  **Cue:** Ops calls it from the Reaper feed about a minute after it moves (F10: QRF COLUMN).
- **Enemy missiles.** Igla MANPADS. About three runs in five: a team in the farm buildings north of
  the road. **Cue:** Warden calls it 40 s before it is in place (F10: MANPADS). About one run in two:
  a hidden gunner in a ditch south of the road. **Cue:** Ops (Reaper) calls the tube 30-60 s
  before it can fire (F10: POSSIBLE MANPADS). No radar SAMs. Every aircraft carries flares.
- **Friendly.** Warden Three-One (eight dismounts, two wounded) and Warden Three-Two (two M2
  HMMWVs and one TOW HMMWV). Dusty 1-1 and 1-2, the detachment's only two serviceable Apaches.
  Dustoff 3-1 with a flight medic. Venom 1-1, two F-16Cs with laser-guided bombs, holding 15 NM
  west at 20,000 ft.

## 2. Mission

Dusty and Venom hold off the militia and the QRF so that Warden can break contact. Dustoff 3-1
lifts Warden's dismounts from a pickup zone behind their own line and clears the plain westbound.

## 3. Execution

1. Dusty and Dustoff start airborne about 7 NM west of the fight. Dusty goes straight in to a
   battle position west of Warden; Dustoff holds 3 NM west of the PZ.
2. Warden clears Dusty hot on the farm wall: gun runs, danger close.
3. When the QRF is called, Dusty switches to Hellfires: the air-defence vehicle first, then the
   lead armour. Ops clears Venom hot on the column 45 s later.
4. After the bombs (or by 9:30 at the latest) Warden breaks contact. The dismounts move 1.3 km west
   to the PZ, a fallow field behind their own line. Warden Three-Two drives 5 km west to the rally
   point on the Binnish road.
5. When Warden reports at the PZ, Dustoff comes in, spends about 90-120 s on the ground, lifts with
   all eight aboard and dashes west at 300 ft. Dusty covers, then escorts. The mission ends when
   Dustoff is clear of the plain.

**What changes each time:** how many militia and what they carry, whether the QRF rolls early (and
reaches the road bend before the bombs) or late, the column's size and its gun, the truck serial,
both missile teams, and whether Warden Three-Two loses a vehicle.

## 4. Support

Ops: Hatay Ops, 251.0 AM (all aircraft). Warden Three-One: 41.5 FM. Reaper (UAV feed) relays
through Ops.

## 5. Command and signal

All stations share one net, so you hear everything. **Abort:** if Dustoff is lost, Ops launches
CSAR and the mission ends as a failure. **Bingo:** not a factor; the mission ends at 20:00 at the
latest. The mission ends automatically about 20 s after Dustoff reaches the egress point.
