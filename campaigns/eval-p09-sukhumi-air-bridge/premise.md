## Pitch

> 1993, Abkhazia. A Mi-8 crew flies an air bridge into a besieged airfield, carrying refugees out
> and ammunition in, under fire from both sides and with fuel always short. Invented crew, real
> geography.

September 1993. The Sochi ceasefire has collapsed, Abkhaz and North Caucasian volunteer forces are
closing on Sukhumi, and the only reliable way in or out of the city is Babushara airfield on the
coast south-east of town. The player commands **Merani 2-1**, a Georgian Mi-8MTV2 of an invented
transport helicopter detachment at Senaki. Every sortie goes in heavy with ammunition and comes
out heavy with refugees and wounded. The besiegers shoot at the helicopters over the Gumista and
the coast; on the way home, the route crosses Mingrelia, where Zviadist irregulars loyal to the
deposed president are in open revolt against the government the crew flies for. Fuel at Senaki is
rationed and at Babushara it may not exist at all.

## Mode rationale

**Mode adapted.** The war in Abkhazia, the fall of Sukhumi (16-27 Sept 1993), the shoot-downs of
airliners at Babushara and the Zviadist revolt in western Georgia are real and are sourced in
`bible.yaml`. The detachment, the crew, every named character and every specific sortie are
invented. Real people are referred to only by office (the Defence Ministry, "the government in
Tbilisi") and never voiced. Timeline compression is noted in `canon_rules`.
